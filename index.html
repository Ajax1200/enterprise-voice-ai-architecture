Architectural Principle & Latency Ceiling

Conversational voice AI systems fail in enterprise sales and customer support when Round-Trip Audio Latency exceeds 600 milliseconds. Human conversational chemistry breaks down once response pauses feel computational rather than spontaneous. To achieve sub-500ms conversational speeds, modern production architectures abandon sequential HTTP request cascades in favor of bidirectional full-duplex WebRTC pipelines. In this paradigm, client audio frames stream constantly into an edge-proxied Voice Activity Detection (VAD) buffer using Silero VAD, feeding token-by-token into continuous Speech-to-Text streaming models such as Deepgram Nova-2 or Whisper-Streaming. Concurrently, inference engines deploy speculative token streaming through ultra-fast LLMs (Groq, Cerebras, or fine-tuned self-hosted vLLM instances), passing initial sentence fragments directly into sub-150ms speech synthesis engines like Cartesia Sonic or ElevenLabs Flash. This overlapping execution model collapses end-to-end voice latency from the historical 2,400ms standard down to an imperceptible 380-480ms.
